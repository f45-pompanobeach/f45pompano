Sales role-plays & lead nurturing

40 points
Unresponsive lead — discovery and rapport Max 5
ROLE-PLAY 1 · 5–7 minutes. Lead inquired two weeks ago, ignored calls and texts, but answers today.
Expected strong answer: Expected: introduces self, seeks permission, asks about goals and barriers before pitching; sounds natural and respectful.
Unresponsive lead — next step Max 5
Continue role-play 1. Prospect is mildly interested but noncommittal. Finish the call and describe the CRM entry.
Expected strong answer: Expected: asks to book a trial or agrees on a specific follow-up time and channel; records interests, objections and next step.
Trial no-return — uncover real reason Max 6
ROLE-PLAY 2 · 7–10 minutes. Trial member attended one class and disappeared. Discover the underlying concern.
Expected strong answer: Expected: asks open questions, listens, uncovers confidence/intensity concern rather than assuming price.
Trial no-return — reassure and book Max 6
Continue role-play 2 after prospect expresses fear of not keeping up.
Expected strong answer: Expected: empathetic response, explains coaching and modifications accurately, proposes a suitable class without pressure.
Post-trial non-conversion follow-up Max 5
Prospect completed three classes but did not join. Describe a respectful two-week follow-up plan.
Expected strong answer: Expected: a few purposeful contacts, personalized messages, CRM notes and respect for opt-outs; no invented discounts.
Slow-day pipeline initiative Max 7
It is 11 AM and urgent tasks are done. Old leads, trial drop-offs and former members remain. What do you do for 2–3 hours?
Expected strong answer: Expected: segments leads, prioritizes outreach, updates CRM and works independently on approved tasks.
No responses — next action and reporting Max 6
Two hours of outreach produce no replies. What do you change and report?
Expected strong answer: Expected: reviews timing/quality, adjusts outreach thoughtfully, records next steps and reports concrete facts.

Reliability & accountability

15 points
8:30 AM ET punctuality Max 8
What could interfere with your agreed 8:30 AM Eastern start, and what backup arrangements do you have?
Expected strong answer: Expected: practical schedule, reliable equipment/internet and prompt communication when problems arise.
Competing demands and consistency Max 7
A hot lead calls while a member has a billing concern and an admin deadline is due. How do you prioritize and track them?
Expected strong answer: Expected: prioritizes time-sensitive work, acknowledges other requests and tracks follow-ups.

Independence & judgment

10 points
Owner unavailable / policy judgment Max 10
A member requests a fee waiver or special price while I am unavailable. What do you say and do?
Expected strong answer: Expected: no unauthorized promises; empathetic response, accurate notes, escalation and follow-through.

Communication & owner working style

10 points
Clear customer and owner communication Max 10
How would you communicate with a prospect wanting a quick answer versus a member who needs to feel heard? How would you raise a decision with me?
Expected strong answer: Expected: concise with prospects, empathetic with members, concise owner updates with facts, options and recommendation.

Work history, owner compatibility & long-term fit

25 points
Schedule and competing commitments Max 5
What weekday blocks can you commit to, including 8:30 AM ET? What weekend availability and other obligations do you have?
Expected strong answer: Expected: specific sustainable hours, realistic availability and honest disclosure of other commitments.
Compensation alignment Max 5
What monthly USD compensation would make this agreed schedule and these responsibilities sustainable for you?
Expected strong answer: Expected: clear compensation expectation tied to hours and responsibilities.
Previous employers and tenure Max 5
Walk me through your last three employers or clients: company, role, start and end dates, how long you stayed and why you left. Any gaps or overlaps?
Expected strong answer: Expected: consistent timeline and candid, professional explanations. Consider short tenures in context rather than penalizing automatically.
Long-term commitment Max 5
What would make you want to stay and grow with one business for several years? What has made you stay in, or leave, previous roles?
Expected strong answer: Expected: realistic conditions for stability and growth, with examples from their history rather than a rehearsed promise.
Working directly with the owner Max 5
We will work together daily. How do you like instructions and feedback? If I change priorities or disagree with your approach, what do you do? Give an example. Also, if I asked your last manager what was sometimes frustrating about working with you, what would they say?
Expected strong answer: Expected: clarifies priorities, communicates respectfully, handles direct feedback, identifies a believable weakness without blaming others, and explains what they changed.
